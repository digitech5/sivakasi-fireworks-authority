Our Fact-Checking Process

Every piece of content on Sivakasi Fireworks Guide goes through a rigorous review process:

  1. Initial Research: Authors gather data from primary sources.
  2. Drafting: Content is written adhering to our style and safety guidelines.
  3. Expert Review: A secondary safety expert reviews the claims (especially regarding storage and handling).
  4. Final Polish: The editorial team ensures readability, clarity, and formatting before publishing.

Our Sources

We rely on authoritative bodies to inform our safety and legal guidelines, including:

  • Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation (PESO)
  • Tamil Nadu Pollution Control Board (TNPCB)
  • Supreme Court of India Directives (on Green Crackers)
  • India Meteorological Department (for weather-related storage tips)
  • National Safety Council of India

How to Submit a Correction

If you spot a factual error, outdated legal information, or broken link on our site, please let us know. We are committed to transparency and will correct confirmed errors promptly.
