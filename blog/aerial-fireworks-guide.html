When you want to truly light up the night, nothing compares to aerial fireworks. These are the giants of the pyrotechnic world—designed to launch high into the sky and erupt into massive canopies of color, light, and sound. From single sky shots to elaborate multi-burst cakes that put on an automated display, aerial fireworks are the grand finale of any serious Diwali celebration.

Because of their size and power, aerial fireworks demand respect. This comprehensive guide will explain the different types of aerials available from Sivakasi, how to build a professional-looking show in your own backyard, and the critical safety measures you must follow.

Types of Aerial Fireworks

Aerial fireworks can generally be divided into two main categories: single shots and multi-shots (cakes).

How Sky Shots Work

A sky shot (or single-shot tube) consists of a single mortar tube containing one aerial shell. When you light the fuse, it ignites a lift charge at the bottom of the tube. This rapid expansion of gas propels the shell high into the air. Simultaneously, a time-delay fuse inside the shell is ignited. Once the shell reaches its apex (usually 100-200 feet up), the internal fuse reaches the burst charge, exploding the shell and sending the colored "stars" flying outward in a perfectly spherical pattern.

Sky shots are fantastic for pacing a show. You can buy them in boxes of 3, 5, or 10 and launch them individually to build suspense.

Multi-Burst Cakes Explained

A "cake" is essentially a cluster of single-shot tubes fused together in a specific sequence. You light one single fuse, and the firework automatically fires multiple shots one after another. Cakes are categorized by their shot count (e.g., 12-shot, 30-shot, 120-shot, 240-shot).

Sivakasi manufacturers design cakes to act as "shows in a box." A good 60-shot cake might start with red and green peonies, transition into whistling comets, and finish with a rapid-fire barrage of crackling silver willows. They offer unparalleled convenience and spectacular visual impact.

Shell Fireworks

Some advanced consumer kits come with reloadable shells. These kits include a durable launch tube (usually fiberglass or heavy cardboard) and a set of individually wrapped shells. You drop one shell into the tube, light the long fuse that hangs out the top, and retreat. After it fires, you clear the tube and load the next one. These often provide the largest, most professional-looking bursts available to consumers.

Display Planning

If you want to move from just "bursting crackers" to hosting a true Diwali fireworks display, planning is key:

  1. Start Small: Begin the evening with ground-based items like flower pots and chakkars to gather the crowd.
  2. Introduce Aerials: Fire a few single sky shots to announce that the main show is starting.
  3. Pacing: Use smaller 12-shot or 30-shot cakes to build momentum. Allow brief pauses between cakes to let the smoke clear.
  4. The Grand Finale: Save your largest, most rapid-firing multi-burst cake (like a 120-shot or 240-shot) for the very end. The goal is to fill the sky completely before the show ends.

Launch Safety

Aerial fireworks are essentially mortars. If a cake or tube falls over while firing, it will shoot flaming projectiles directly at the audience. Follow our Safety Guide strictly.

Critical Rule: NEVER look down the tube of a sky shot or cake to see if it is lit. If it fires while your face is above it, the result will be fatal.

Always place cakes and tubes on flat, hard ground. Do not put them on grass or uneven dirt. For multi-shot cakes, it is highly recommended to place heavy bricks or cement blocks on at least two sides of the cake to prevent it from tipping over due to its own recoil.

Minimum Distances

Aerial fireworks require significant clearance:

  • Overhead: You must have a clear view of the sky. No trees, no power lines, no awnings, and no overhanging balconies.
  • Spectator Distance: For smaller cakes and sky shots, spectators should be at least 25-30 meters (75-100 feet) away. For massive display cakes, increase this to 50 meters.

Weather Considerations

Wind is the enemy of aerial fireworks. If there are strong winds, the wind will carry the flaming debris horizontally as it falls, potentially landing on roofs, dry grass, or people. If it is a windy Diwali night, cancel the aerial display and stick to ground-based fireworks. Safety must always come first.

Price Guide

Aerial fireworks are the most expensive category, but they offer the biggest spectacle:

  • Single Sky Shots (Box of 3-5): ₹400 - ₹800
  • Small Cakes (12 - 30 shots): ₹600 - ₹1,500
  • Medium Cakes (60 - 120 shots): ₹2,000 - ₹5,000
  • Massive Display Cakes (240+ shots): ₹6,000 - ₹15,000+

Building a Show

When selecting your aerials, pay attention to the effects listed on the box. Try to mix different colors and shapes. A show entirely of red peonies gets boring; mix in some glittering willows, crackling comets, and whistling tails. By thoughtfully selecting and sequencing your aerial fireworks, you can create a Diwali night that your friends and neighbors will remember for years.

FAQ

How high do sky shots go?

Consumer aerial sky shots and cakes from Sivakasi typically reach heights of 100 to 250 feet depending on the tube size.

Why did my multi-shot cake stop halfway?

This can happen if the internal fusing breaks or gets damp. Never look into the tubes to check. Wait 30 minutes and then soak it in water.

How do I secure an aerial cake?

Place it on flat concrete and bracket it with heavy bricks on at least two sides to prevent it from tipping over while firing.

Are aerial fireworks legal in residential areas?

It depends on local regulations, noise curfews, and available open space. You must have adequate overhead clearance away from buildings and trees.

What does a 30-shot cake mean?

It means the firework contains 30 individual tubes bundled together. Once lit, it will automatically shoot 30 aerial bursts in sequence.

Can I use aerial fireworks when it's windy?

No. Strong winds can blow the flaming debris horizontally into buildings, trees, or crowds. Save aerials for calm nights.

Conclusion

Aerial fireworks transform a simple celebration into a majestic event. With their breathtaking heights and vibrant bursts, they capture the true spirit of festivity. By investing in quality products and adhering to strict safety protocols, you can safely choreograph a dazzling sky show that will leave everyone in awe.
