Types of Sound Crackers

From atom bombs to multi-wala string crackers, sound crackers remain a traditional favorite. They are designed primarily for acoustic impact rather than visual display.

How Loud is Too Loud (dB Guide)

Normal conversation is 60 dB. Sound crackers can exceed 120 dB. Anything above 85 dB for prolonged periods can cause hearing damage.

Legal Noise Limits in India

The Supreme Court of India mandates that fireworks must not exceed 125 dB(AI) or 145 dB(C)pk at a distance of 4 meters. Nighttime curfews (typically 10 PM to 6 AM) strictly forbid their use.

Health Risks of Loud Crackers

Sudden acoustic trauma can cause tinnitus or eardrum rupture. Smoke generated by string crackers also poses severe respiratory risks.

Warning: Always wear earplugs if you are lighting heavy sound crackers.

Impact on Animals and Pets

Pets have sensitive hearing. The sudden booms cause extreme panic and anxiety. Keep pets indoors in a quiet, well-insulated room.

Safety Distances

Always maintain a minimum distance of 20 feet from any exploding sound cracker. Never light them inside metal or glass containers.

Who Should Avoid Sound Crackers

Pregnant women, infants, the elderly, and individuals with heart conditions or PTSD should steer clear of sound cracker zones.

Tip: Check our Safety Guide for more details on vulnerable groups.

Legal vs Illegal Sound Levels

Authentic Sivakasi brands strictly adhere to PESO regulations. Unbranded or smuggled crackers often violate these limits and are incredibly dangerous.

Eco-Alternatives

Consider green crackers, which have a modified chemical composition that reduces both noise and emission levels by 30%.

Responsible Use

Use sound crackers sparingly. Balance your celebration with visual fireworks to reduce the overall noise footprint in your neighborhood.

Note: Always sweep up the paper debris left behind by string crackers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are sound crackers banned?

Not universally, but high-decibel ones are restricted, and curfews apply.

What is the legal dB limit?

125 decibels at a 4-meter distance.

How to calm pets during Diwali?

Keep them indoors, close windows, and play soothing background music.

Can sound crackers damage hearing?

Yes, permanent tinnitus or hearing loss can occur from close exposure.

What are green sound crackers?

Crackers developed by CSIR-NEERI that produce less noise and smoke.

Can I light bombs on the street?

It is dangerous and often illegal to light explosives on public thoroughfares.

Conclusion

Sound crackers are deeply ingrained in tradition, but they require immense responsibility. Respect legal limits, protect vulnerable groups, and prioritize safety.
