Frequently Asked Questions

1. Where can I buy original Sivakasi crackers online? +
We recommend purchasing directly from reputed Sivakasi manufacturers or authorized dealers online. To ensure authenticity and safety, always verify the dealer's credentials.
2. What are the safest fireworks for kids? +
Sparklers, ground chakkars, flower pots, and small fountains are generally considered safest for supervised kids. Always ensure adult supervision and keep a bucket of water nearby.
3. How should I store crackers safely at home? +
Store crackers in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, moisture, and any sources of heat or fire. Keep them in their original packaging and out of reach of children.
4. What is the best time to buy Diwali crackers? +
The best time to buy is usually 3-4 weeks before Diwali. This ensures you get the best stock and early-bird discounts before the peak season rush.
5. Are online crackers cheaper than local shops? +
Yes, buying online directly from Sivakasi dealers usually eliminates middlemen margins, offering wholesale prices that are often 30-50% cheaper than local retail shops.
6. What fireworks are banned in India? +
Fireworks containing barium salts, lithium, arsenic, antimony, lead, and mercury are banned. Only green crackers with reduced emissions are legally permitted in many regions.
7. How do I know if Sivakasi crackers are genuine? +
Genuine Sivakasi crackers feature clear manufacturer details, safety warnings, and the mandatory PESO (Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation) license number printed on the box.
8. What safety equipment do I need for Diwali? +
Keep buckets of water and sand, a first aid kit, cotton clothing, closed footwear, and safety goggles. An agarbatti (incense stick) or sparkler is safer for lighting than a direct lighter.
