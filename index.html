⚠️ Essential Safety Rules Before You Light Any Fireworks

01

Outdoor Use Only

Always light fireworks in clear, open areas away from buildings, trees, and vehicles.

02

Keep Water Ready

Have a bucket of water or sand and a garden hose nearby in case of emergencies.

03

Supervise Children

Never allow young children to handle or light fireworks without adult supervision.

04

One at a Time

Light only one firework at a time and move back immediately to a safe distance.

05

Never Relight

If a firework fails to ignite, do not try to relight it. Wait 20 minutes, then soak it in water.

06

Proper Disposal

Soak all used fireworks in water before disposing of them in a metal trash can.
