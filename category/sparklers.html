About Sparklers

Sparklers are the universal symbol of celebration. From standard 10cm wire sparklers to massive 50cm variants, they bring joy to Diwali, weddings, and birthdays alike. The classic 'electric' sparklers provide a brilliant white shower, while color sparklers (red, green, gold) add variety. Lollipop sparklers and root sparklers offer thicker, longer-lasting burns. They are the perfect entry-level firework for children (under close supervision) to participate in the festivities safely.

Sparklers consist of a stiff wire core coated in a thick paste of pyrotechnic composition. This paste contains a fuel, an oxidizer, and metal powders (like aluminum, iron, or titanium) that create the iconic branching sparks when they burn in the air. A binder holds it all together. The slow, controlled burn rate allows for a sustained, hand-held display that doesn't produce percussive shockwaves, making them exceptionally popular in dense urban settings.

What to Look For When Buying

  • Buy longer sparklers (30cm+) for younger children to keep the sparks away from hands.
  • Store in a perfectly dry place; damp sparklers will simply refuse to light.
  • Thicker chemical coatings usually mean a longer, brighter burn time.
  • Electric (white) sparklers typically burn brighter than color ones due to titanium/aluminum.
  • Always check the wire quality—it shouldn't bend easily under normal pressure.

Safety Tips for Sparklers

  • Hold the sparkler at arm's length, pointing away from the face and body.
  • Only light one sparkler at a time. Lighting a bundle creates a massive hazard.
  • Never wave them aggressively near other people or run with them.
  • Wear closed-toe shoes to protect feet from falling hot sparks.
  • Immediately drop spent wires into a bucket of water; they remain hot for minutes.

FAQ

Why is the leftover wire dangerous?

The metal wire core stays at over 300°C for several minutes after the sparks stop. Always drop it in water.

Can I use sparklers indoors for a birthday?

Standard fireworks sparklers produce too much smoke. Use specialized 'indoor' or 'ice fountain' sparklers for cakes.

Where to buy high-quality sparklers?

What is an 'Electric' sparkler?

It refers to a sparkler that uses aluminum/titanium to produce bright, white, electric-looking sparks.
