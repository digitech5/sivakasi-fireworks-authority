About Flower Pots

Flower pots, also known as fountains, are a staple of Indian festivals. From small pots suitable for tight spaces to massive conical fountains that erupt into a 15-foot shower of gold and silver sparks, they are beloved for their stunning, continuous displays. Color-changing varieties and those that crackle add layers of excitement. They are widely considered one of the safest fireworks, making them perfect for family celebrations and younger participants (aged 8+).

A flower pot is essentially a cone-shaped tube packed tightly with a pyrotechnic mixture. As it burns from top to bottom, the increasing surface area of the cone means more powder burns simultaneously, causing the shower of sparks to grow taller and wider. The sparks are typically created by coarse metal powders like iron or titanium burning in the air. The quality of the cone's casing ensures it withstands the internal pressure without bursting.

What to Look For When Buying

  • Bigger cones equal taller and longer-lasting displays, so check dimensions.
  • Check the base to ensure it sits perfectly flat and stable on the ground.
  • Look for 'crackling' or 'color-changing' variants for an auditory and visual bonus.
  • Buy in bulk boxes for the best per-piece value during Diwali.
  • Avoid pots with loose powder leaking from the top seal.

Safety Tips for Flower Pots

  • Place only on flat, paved surfaces, never on uneven grass or dirt.
  • Light the tip at arm's length using a long agarbatti or sparkler.
  • Never hold a flower pot while it is burning under any circumstances.
  • Keep a safe distance of at least 10 feet.
  • Do not try to reignite if it stops halfway; douse it in water.

FAQ

Can I light flower pots indoors?

Absolutely not. The sparks and smoke require outdoor ventilation and clearance.

Why did my flower pot burst?

A burst cone means moisture weakened the casing or it was a counterfeit product. Always buy authentic Sivakasi brands.

Are flower pots safe for 8-year-olds?

Yes, provided an adult lights it and the child watches from a safe distance of 10+ feet.

Where to buy authentic flower pots?

We recommend authorized Sivakasi dealers like RedCrackers.net.

How long do they burn?

Giant pots can burn up to 2 minutes, while smaller ones last 30-45 seconds.
