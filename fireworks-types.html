Types of Fireworks in India

From traditional sparklers to grand aerial multi-shots, discover the complete encyclopedia of Sivakasi fireworks.

1. Sparklers (Kambi Mathappu)

Safety: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

The quintessential Diwali staple. Sparklers consist of a metal wire coated with a chemical paste that burns slowly, emitting brilliant sparks. They come in various sizes (7cm to 50cm) and styles like electric, color, and crackling.

Who Can Use Kids (5+ with supervision)
Occasion Diwali, Weddings, Parties
Price Range ₹ - ₹₹

2. Flower Pots / Fountains (Changu Chakkaram)

Safety: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Cone or cylinder-shaped fireworks placed on the ground. When lit, they erupt into a shower of sparks resembling a fountain or flower pot. Available in variations like Color, Gold, and Ashoka.

Who Can Use 8+ (Adult lighting recommended)
Occasion Diwali, Temple Festivals
Price Range ₹₹ - ₹₹₹

3. Ground Chakkars (Zamin Chakkar)

Safety: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Small circular discs that spin rapidly on the ground when lit, creating a halo of fire. Requires a smooth, flat surface to work properly. Variants include Big, Special, and Deluxe sizes.

Who Can Use 8+ (Adult lighting recommended)
Occasion Diwali
Price Range ₹ - ₹₹

4. Rockets

Safety: ⭐⭐

Attached to a stick, rockets shoot into the air before bursting or whistling. Must be launched from a secure bottle or pipe, never directly from the ground or hand. Requires open skies.

Who Can Use Adults Only (16+)
Occasion Diwali, New Year
Price Range ₹₹ - ₹₹₹

5. Aerial Fancy / Multi-Shots (Sky Shots)

Safety: ⭐⭐⭐

A single box containing multiple tubes that shoot synchronized shells into the air, creating massive color patterns (peonies, willows, chrysanthemums). Ranging from 12 shots to 500+ shots.

Who Can Use Adults Only
Occasion Grand Finales, Weddings, NYE
Price Range ₹₹₹ - ₹₹₹₹₹

6. Sound Crackers (Atom Bombs, Hydro Bombs)

Safety: ⭐⭐

Designed solely for a loud concussive blast. Wrapped tightly in jute or paper. Due to noise regulations, their chemical composition is strictly regulated to stay under 125 decibels.

Who Can Use Adults Only
Occasion Daytime Diwali
Price Range ₹ - ₹₹

7. Garlands (Walas / Lar)

Safety: ⭐⭐

Hundreds or thousands of small crackers tied together on a single string. Sizes range from 100-wala to 10,000-wala. Known for creating an extended, continuous loud noise.

Who Can Use Adults Only
Occasion Processions, Daytime Diwali
Price Range ₹₹ - ₹₹₹₹

8. Kids Novelty (Snakes, Pop-Pops, Magic Matches)

Safety: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Very low intensity items. Pop-pops detonate with a small snap when thrown. Black snakes expand into a long ash cylinder when lit. Highly popular for young children as they produce no loud bangs.

Who Can Use Kids (under adult supervision)
Occasion Anytime
Price Range ₹
