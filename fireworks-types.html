Complete Guide to All Types of Indian Fireworks

From rockets and sparklers to fancy sky shots — understand every type before you buy online

The world of Indian fireworks is vast and diverse, with traditional cracker makers in Sivakasi introducing new, innovative variations every year. Understanding the different categories is crucial not only for getting the best value for your money but also for ensuring the safety of your family during festival celebrations.

Whether you're looking for mild, colorful displays suitable for children or loud, spectacular sky shows that light up the night, knowing what each type does will help you build the perfect assortment. In this guide, we break down all the major categories of fireworks available in the Indian market today.

🎇
Low Risk Best For: Kids (with supervision)

Sparklers (Phuljadi)

The most common and beloved firework. A wire coated with chemical composition that emits a shower of colored sparks when lit. Available in various lengths (7cm to 50cm) and colors like gold, silver, red, and green.

Average Price: ₹20 - ₹200 per box

View Sparklers Guide
🏺
Medium Risk Best For: Families

Flower Pots (Anaar)

Cone-shaped fireworks placed on the ground that shoot a fountain of sparks upward. Known for their brilliant display of colors and height. Larger variants can reach up to 10-15 feet in height.

Average Price: ₹50 - ₹500 per box

View Flower Pots Guide
🌀
Medium Risk Best For: Everyone

Ground Chakkars

Flat, circular fireworks that spin rapidly on the ground, creating a wheel of fire. They emit very little sound and are purely visual. Requires a smooth, flat surface to perform effectively.

Average Price: ₹40 - ₹150 per box

View Chakkars Guide
🚀
High Risk Best For: Open Spaces

Sky Rockets

Mounted on a stick, these shoot high into the air before exploding with a bang or a shower of colors. Must be launched from a stable bottle or pipe in open areas away from trees and power lines.

Average Price: ₹80 - ₹400 per box

View Rockets Guide
💣
High Risk Best For: Loud Noise

Atom Bombs

Tightly wrapped balls of explosive powder wrapped in jute string (sutli). Known for producing an extremely loud bang. Must be kept at a considerable distance as they can cause severe ear damage.

Average Price: ₹50 - ₹150 per box

View Bombs Guide
🧶
Medium Risk Best For: Long Duration

Garlands (Ladi)

Multiple small firecrackers tied together in a chain, ranging from 100 to 10,000+ pieces. When lit, they produce a continuous series of rapid explosions. Very common in weddings and major celebrations.

Average Price: ₹100 - ₹5000+ per piece

View Garlands Guide
🎆
Medium Risk Best For: Night Sky Display

Fancy Fireworks (Shots)

Multi-shot cakes or single aerial shells that shoot up into the sky and burst into massive, colorful patterns like peonies, willows, or crackling stars. The ultimate visual treat for large gatherings.

Average Price: ₹500 - ₹5000 per box

View Fancy Shots Guide
🎁
Varies Best For: Gifting

Gift Box Sets

Pre-packaged assortments containing a variety of fireworks like sparklers, flower pots, chakkars, and mild sound crackers. Perfect for corporate gifting or families wanting a ready-made collection.

Average Price: ₹300 - ₹3000 per box

View Gift Boxes Guide
💫
Low Risk Best For: Novelty

Ground Spinners

Similar to chakkars but with different effects. They might whistle, change colors, or pop as they spin. Often shaped as small cylinders or discs that provide an entertaining visual display.

Average Price: ₹50 - ₹200 per box

View Spinners Guide
✏️
Low Risk Best For: Kids (with supervision)

Pencil Crackers

Long, thin tubes that emit bright, colored flames resembling a giant match or flare. They are low on noise and very safe for older children to hold when supervised.

Average Price: ₹60 - ₹150 per box

View Pencils Guide
⭐
Medium Risk Best For: Daytime/Evening

Star Bombs (Bullet Crackers)

Small, cylindrical firecrackers that produce a sharp, loud pop. Often sold in strips or small boxes. Much safer than atom bombs but still require careful handling.

Average Price: ₹30 - ₹100 per box

View Star Bombs Guide
⚡
Medium Risk Best For: Traditional Noise

Bijli Crackers

Tiny red crackers strung together. When lit individually or in a small bunch, they make a sharp snapping sound. A very traditional staple of Diwali celebrations.

Average Price: ₹20 - ₹60 per bundle

Expert Advice

Buying Tips by Category

For Kids & Toddlers

  • Stick strictly to sparklers, magic snakes, and pencil crackers.
  • Buy extra-long sparklers to keep sparks away from their hands.
  • Avoid any noise-making crackers completely.

For Spectacular Displays

  • Invest in multi-shot cakes (30, 60, or 120 shots) for a professional look.
  • Buy large flower pots (Asoka or Giant size) for dramatic ground effects.
  • Ensure you have wide open space before buying sky rockets.

For Budget Buyers

  • Gift boxes offer the best variety-to-price ratio.
  • Standard flower pots and chakkars are very cost-effective.
  • Buy garlands instead of individual bombs for more noise per rupee.
