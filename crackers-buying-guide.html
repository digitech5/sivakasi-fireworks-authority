1

Find PESO-licensed Sellers

Ensure the online store has a valid Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation (PESO) license. This guarantees they are legally authorized to sell and ship fireworks.

2

Compare Prices & Discounts

Check multiple genuine Sivakasi portals. Look for flat discounts (typically 40-80% during festival season) on the printed MRP.

3

Choose Your Crackers Wisely

Select items based on who will be using them. Consider gift boxes for variety, sparklers and chakkars for kids, and aerial shells for adults.

4

Verify Pincode Delivery

Before building a large cart, enter your pincode to ensure their specialized logistics partners can deliver fireworks to your location.

5

Safe & Secure Payment

Use secure payment gateways. Pay via UPI, Credit/Debit cards, or Net Banking. Beware of sellers asking for direct unverified bank transfers.

Receive & Inspect

Upon delivery, inspect the boxes for any transit damage. Store them immediately in a cool, dry place away from children.
