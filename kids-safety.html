Age-appropriate fireworks, supervision rules, and essential safety tips to keep children safe this festival season

Kid-Friendly Choices

Safe Fireworks for Kids

🎇

Long Sparklers

Choose extra-long (50cm) sparklers to keep sparks further away from hands and face.

🏺

Preparation

Protective Equipment Checklist

  • ✅ Safety Goggles: Essential for protecting eyes from sparks.
  • ✅ Pure Cotton Clothing: Synthetics melt; cotton burns slowly.
  • ✅ Closed-toe Shoes: Protect feet from dropped sparklers.
  • ✅ Ear Plugs: Important for younger kids near loud crackers.
  • ✅ Long Agarbatti: Keeps face away from fuse when lighting.
  • ✅ Bucket of Water: Immediately available for dropping used sparklers.
  • ✅ First Aid Kit: With burn ointment and sterile gauze.
  • ✅ Fire Extinguisher: Keep nearby for emergencies.

Parent Supervision Guide

  1. One-on-One: Assign one adult to directly supervise no more than two children.
  2. Never Inside: Ensure all fireworks are lit outdoors in clear spaces.
  3. One at a Time: Light only one firecracker at a time to maintain control.
  4. No Relighting: Teach kids never to approach or attempt to relight a "dud" firework.
  5. Safe Distance: Establish a clear "viewing line" that kids are not allowed to cross.

🚨 Emergency Procedures

If a child is injured, follow these steps immediately:

For Burns

Cool the burn under cold running water for at least 10 minutes. Do NOT apply ice, butter, or toothpaste. Cover loosely with sterile gauze.

Eye Injuries

Do NOT rub the eye or attempt to flush it out. Cover it with a clean pad and seek immediate medical attention.

Clothing Fire

Instruct the child to STOP, DROP, and ROLL. Smother the flames with a heavy blanket if available.
