Fireworks Types

Sparklers are hand-held sticks that emit colored sparks. Fancy fireworks are ground or aerial devices that produce elaborate visual effects, colors, and sounds.

Sky rockets are aerial fireworks propelled into the sky by a motor, exploding at an altitude to create wide starbursts or noise effects.

Ground chakkars (spinners) are circular fireworks placed on a flat surface that spin rapidly, emitting sparks and colors in a wheel formation.

Flower pots (anars) are conical fireworks that erupt into a shower of sparks. Fountains are similar but can come in various shapes and have longer durations or multi-color changes.

Aerial fireworks are devices designed to be launched into the sky before exploding. This includes rockets, multi-shots (cakes), and shells.
