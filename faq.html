Buying Crackers Online

Where is the best place to buy Sivakasi crackers online?
The best place to buy is directly from authorized Sivakasi dealers and manufacturers online. We suggest looking for portals with clear PESO licenses, good reviews, and secure payment options.
How do I know if online crackers are genuine Sivakasi products?
Genuine products will have the manufacturer's address in Sivakasi, a valid PESO license number printed on the box, proper safety warnings, and high-quality packaging.
What is the minimum order for online crackers?
Most online sellers from Sivakasi require a minimum order value ranging from ₹2000 to ₹3000 to justify the transportation costs of explosives.
How are crackers delivered safely?
Crackers are shipped via specialized transport services certified for handling explosives, packed securely in corrugated boxes to prevent damage and moisture exposure.

Fireworks Types

What is the difference between sparklers and fancy fireworks?
Sparklers are hand-held sticks that emit sparks, while fancy fireworks (aerials) shoot into the sky and burst into colorful patterns.
What are sky shots and how do they work?
Sky shots are aerial fireworks that launch a shell high into the air before bursting into visual effects. They must be placed on flat ground and secured properly.
What are ground chakkars?
Ground chakkars are spinning fireworks that rotate rapidly on the ground, creating a circular shower of sparks. They require a flat, hard surface to spin properly.
What is the difference between flower pots and fountains?
Flower pots are cone-shaped devices that erupt into a shower of sparks (like a volcano). Fountains are similar but can come in various shapes and have longer durations.
What are aerial fireworks?
Aerial fireworks include rockets, multishots, and bomb shells that propel into the air before exploding to produce light and sound effects.

Diwali Safety

What are the basic fireworks safety rules?
Keep water nearby, light one firework at a time, wear cotton clothes, maintain a safe distance, and never relight a dud firework.
What should I do if a cracker doesn't explode?
Wait at least 20 minutes from a distance. Then approach it and douse it thoroughly with water. Never attempt to relight it.
How far should I stand from fireworks?
For ground fireworks, maintain at least a 15-foot distance. For aerial fireworks, maintain a minimum of 30 to 40 feet of clearance.
Is it safe to light crackers indoors?
No. Never light fireworks indoors. All fireworks must be used outdoors in open, well-ventilated areas away from flammable materials.
What first aid should I know for fireworks burns?
Run cool (not cold) water over the burn for 10-15 minutes. Do not apply ice, butter, or ointments. Seek medical attention immediately for severe burns.

Kids Safety

What age is safe for children to use sparklers?
Children should generally be at least 5 years old to handle sparklers, and only under strict adult supervision. Sparklers burn at very high temperatures.
What fireworks are safe for kids under 10?
Small sparklers, glow worms, and mild ground spinners are best. Kids should mostly watch from a safe distance rather than handling fireworks.
Should children wear protective clothing?
Yes, children (and adults) should wear well-fitting, non-synthetic, pure cotton clothes and closed-toe shoes. Avoid loose, flowing garments.
Can children light fireworks themselves?
No, children should never light fireworks themselves. An adult should always be the one lighting the fuse.
What should I do if a child is injured?
Immediately administer proper first aid (cool water for burns) and rush to the nearest hospital or emergency room.

Storage & Handling

How should I store unused crackers?
Store them in a sealed container in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, moisture, heaters, and electrical outlets.
How long can crackers be stored?
If stored properly in a dry environment, crackers can last 1 to 2 years, though they are best used within the same season for optimal performance.
What temperature is safe for cracker storage?
Room temperature (around 20-25°C) is safe. Avoid extreme heat environments like unventilated sheds or attics during summer.
Can crackers get damaged by humidity?
Yes, humidity is the biggest enemy of fireworks. Moisture dampens the powder, leading to duds or unpredictable explosions. Keep them extremely dry.
How should I dispose of unused crackers?
Submerge them completely in a bucket of water for at least 15-30 minutes until thoroughly soaked, then dispose of them with regular household trash.

Legal & Regulations

Which fireworks are banned in India?
Traditional fireworks using barium, lithium, arsenic, and antimony are banned by the Supreme Court. Only 'Green Crackers' with reduced emissions are allowed.
Are there noise level restrictions for crackers?
Yes, firecrackers exceeding a specific decibel limit (usually 125 dB at a 4-meter distance) are strictly banned across India.
What are the legal timings to burst crackers?
Timings vary by state, but the Supreme Court generally mandates bursting crackers only during designated 2-hour windows (e.g., 8 PM to 10 PM) during festivals.
Do I need a license to buy crackers?
Consumers do not need a license to buy crackers for personal use. However, sellers and distributors must possess valid PESO licenses.
Are eco-friendly crackers mandatory?
In many states and major metro areas (like Delhi NCR), only certified Green Crackers are legally permitted to be sold and used.
