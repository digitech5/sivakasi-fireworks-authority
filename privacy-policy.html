Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

1. Introduction & Who We Are

Welcome to the Sivakasi Fireworks Guide. We are India's premier educational platform dedicated to providing trustworthy information about Sivakasi fireworks, safe festival celebrations, cracker types, and responsible buying practices. We recognize the importance of your privacy and are deeply committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, the rationale behind collecting it, and the stringent measures we take to safeguard it.

When you visit https://sivakasi-fireworks.in, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy. Our goal is to ensure you can confidently browse our educational guides, safety checklists, and blog articles, knowing that your data privacy is respected and prioritized. We are not a direct fireworks seller, but an informational hub aimed at helping you make safer, better-informed choices for your family's celebrations.

2. Information We Collect

To provide you with the most relevant and high-quality content, we collect information in a few different ways. First, we collect information that you voluntarily provide to us. For example, if you subscribe to our Diwali Festival Newsletter, we collect your name and email address. If you reach out to us via our contact form, we collect the details you choose to share, such as your email and the contents of your message.

Second, we automatically collect certain technical information when you navigate our website. This includes your IP address, browser type, device information (such as whether you are using a mobile phone or desktop), and details about how you interact with our pages (which articles you read, how long you stay on a page, and the links you click). We gather this data using standard internet technologies like cookies, web beacons, and server logs.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly used to improve your experience on our platform and deliver greater value. We use your data to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Sivakasi Fireworks Guide website.
  • Analyze reading habits to understand which topics (like safety tips or buying guides) resonate most, allowing us to produce better content.
  • Send you our occasional newsletter containing seasonal tips, safety updates, and curated articles, provided you have explicitly opted in.
  • Respond to your inquiries, comments, or support requests in a timely manner.
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues or fraudulent activities to keep our website secure.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing companies. The data is utilized purely to enhance the educational value of our platform.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small data files stored on your device that help us remember your preferences and understand how you use our site. We use both session cookies (which expire when you close your browser) and persistent cookies (which remain on your device until deleted).

These cookies enable essential website functions and help us track analytics. For instance, a cookie helps us know if you've already dismissed a popup message so you aren't annoyed by it repeatedly. You have complete control over cookies; most web browsers allow you to manage or disable them entirely. However, please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality or user experience of certain features on our website.

5. Third-Party Services

We leverage a select number of trusted third-party services to help operate our website effectively. For example, we use Google Analytics to track website traffic, measure the popularity of specific guides, and understand geographic demographics (like which states our readers are visiting from). Google Analytics collects data via cookies and operates under its own privacy policies.

If you subscribe to our newsletter, your email is processed by our secure email marketing provider. Additionally, we may feature external links to reputable resources or trusted partners (for example, RedCrackers.net). When you click an external link, you leave our site, and our privacy policy no longer applies. We encourage you to read the privacy statements of any third-party websites you visit.

6. Your Data Privacy Rights

We believe in empowering you with control over your personal data. Regardless of where you live, we strive to uphold high standards of privacy, inspired by global frameworks like the GDPR and local frameworks such as the Indian Information Technology Act. You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we currently hold about you (such as your newsletter subscription details).
  • Correction: Request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Deletion: Request that we erase your personal data from our systems (often called the "right to be forgotten").
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link at the bottom of any email we send.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided in the final section of this policy.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as is strictly necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy. For example, if you subscribe to our newsletter, we keep your email address until you choose to unsubscribe. Analytic data, which is largely anonymized and aggregated, is typically retained for up to 26 months to help us compare year-over-year festival trends. Once data is no longer necessary, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

8. Security Measures

The security of your data is of paramount importance to us. We employ standard industry safeguards to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your personal information. Our website forces HTTPS encryption, meaning any data transmitted between your browser and our servers is scrambled and secure.

We also regularly update our software, utilize secure web hosting environments, and limit access to personal data to authorized personnel only. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

While our website features guides on "Kids Fireworks Safety" for parents, the website itself is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information (e.g., by signing up for a newsletter), please contact us immediately so that we can take necessary actions to remove such information from our databases.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The digital landscape and privacy regulations are continually evolving. As such, we may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technologies, or legal requirements. When we make updates, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information. Your continued use of the website following the posting of any changes indicates your acceptance of those changes.

11. Contact for Privacy Concerns

Your trust is crucial to us. If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, or if you would like to exercise your data rights, please do not hesitate to contact our privacy team.

Email: privacy@sivakasi-fireworks.in
Mailing Address: Sivakasi Fireworks Guide Editorial Team, Sivakasi, Tamil Nadu, India.

We are dedicated to resolving any privacy concerns quickly, transparently, and respectfully.
